# IT265 Module 2: Idea Bank

Use this sheet during the concept workshop. Link the completed document from your workshop index; it supports the journal and treatment rather than adding another graded submission.

List possible concepts before choosing one. Mark each as **ready to pitch**, **unclear loop**, **scope risk**, or **save for later**. Shortlist two or three ideas for the pitch round.

| Working title | Repeated player decision or action | Category | Reason or open question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cat Cafe Chaos | The player has to prioritize which customer or cat need to handle first while keeping both customers and unpredictable cats happy. | Time Management / Simulation | **Ready to pitch.** The core loop is clear, but the amount of simultaneous requests needs to be tested so the game feels challenging without becoming overwhelming. |
| Cozy Garden | The player grows and takes care of plants by deciding which plants need water, sunlight, or attention first. | Simulation / Cozy | **Ready to pitch.** The concept is relaxing and simple, but the game needs enough decisions to remain engaging rather than becoming repetitive. |
| Lost & Found | The player searches for misplaced objects and decides where each item belongs while helping different people find their belongings. | Exploration / Puzzle | **Ready to pitch.** The idea has a simple search-and-decision loop, but the difficulty and variety of objects would need to be tested. |
