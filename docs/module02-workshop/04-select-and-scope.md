**Selected concept and reason:**  
I selected **Cat Cafe Chaos** because it has the clearest combination of a repeated decision, immediate pressure, and a playful theme. The player constantly has to decide what needs attention first while balancing the needs of customers and cats. The concept also has a manageable first prototype that can test the central mechanic without requiring a complete digital game.

**Feedback that changed or confirmed my choice, if received (otherwise say unavailable):**  
Peer feedback was **unavailable**. No actual feedback notes were received, so there is no peer feedback to use as evidence for the selection. The choice was therefore based on comparing the concepts and identifying which one had the clearest recurring decision and most feasible prototype.

**Other ideas to keep for later:**  
I would keep **Cozy Garden** and **Lost & Found** as alternative concepts for possible future projects. Cozy Garden could be developed into a more relaxing simulation, while Lost & Found could become a larger exploration or puzzle game.

**First physical prototype boundary:**  
The first prototype will only test the café's prioritization system. It will include a simple café layout, customer and cat request cards, task cards, happiness or patience values, and a short timer or turn system. The prototype will focus on whether players can recognize competing needs and make meaningful choices about what to handle first.

**One feature to defer:**  
I will defer advanced cat behavior and additional café management systems such as purchasing upgrades, decorating the café, or managing a larger menu. These features can be considered later if the basic prioritization loop works.

**One risk to test next:**  
The main risk is whether the amount of simultaneous pressure feels challenging without becoming overwhelming. The next prototype should test how many customer and cat requests a player can reasonably manage at one time and whether choosing between them feels meaningful.
