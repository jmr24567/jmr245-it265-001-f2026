## Vision Statement
*What is the mission of your solo game studio? How does it align with creating and analyzing innovative game architectures?*

> The mission of Cloudberry Interactive is to create cozy games that allow players to decompress and enjoy low-pressure gameplay. The studio focuses on simple but satisfying mechanics, thoughtful game systems, and environments that encourage players to slow down, explore, and enjoy the experience at their own pace.

## Unique Selling Point (USP)
*What will make your games stand out from others, particularly in the indie/board game space?*

> Cloudberry Interactive focuses on creating games where simple actions have satisfying and meaningful results. Rather than relying on high difficulty or constant competition, our games encourage players to explore, care for their surroundings, and find enjoyment in repetitive but rewarding activities. The goal is to create games that feel relaxing while still providing enough mechanics and progression to keep players engaged.

## Additional Notes
*Include any other ideas, inspirations, or thoughts related to your studio or project.*

> Cloudberry Interactive is inspired by games that allow players to slow down and enjoy simple activities without feeling pressured to constantly progress. The studio could explore mechanics such as collecting, gardening, decorating, caring for animals, organizing, exploration, and other satisfying repetitive activities. Future games could experiment with how small interactions and simple mechanics can create a relaxing experience. Although the games are designed to be approachable, they can still include depth through progression systems, resource management, player choice, and interconnected mechanics.
